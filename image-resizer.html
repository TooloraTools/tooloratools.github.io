How to Resize an Image

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Select a preset size or choose Custom Size.
  3. Enter the required width and height if needed.
  4. Select the output format and image quality.
  5. Click Resize Image and download your result.

Your image is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to our server.

How to Resize an Image

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Enter your required width and height in pixels.
  3. Keep aspect ratio enabled to avoid stretching.
  4. Choose the output format and image quality.
  5. Tap Resize Image, then download the result.

Your image is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to our server.

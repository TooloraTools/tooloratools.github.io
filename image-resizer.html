How to Resize an Image

  1. Upload an image from your device.
  2. Choose a platform or search for a preset.
  3. Select Fit, Crop or Stretch.
  4. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP as your output format.
  5. Tap Resize Image, check the preview and download your image.

Preset dimensions are common recommendations and may change as platform requirements are updated. Some platforms display square profile images inside a circle.
